Part 3 · Grammar in context: Present perfect vs past simple

When do we use it?

Present perfect: время не названо, важна связь с настоящим (опыт, результат, «пока не закончилось»).
Past simple: законченное действие, время названо или понятно.

Structure: have/has + V3 · V2

I've met some interesting people this month. (месяц ещё идёт)
I met an interesting guy yesterday. (вчера, закончено)
Have you ever been to a language café? · When did you go?

Part 4 · Reading (B1+)

The art of small talk

Many people say they hate small talk, but it matters more than we think. A short chat about the weather or the weekend is rarely about the topic. It tells the other person, "I'm friendly, and I'm happy to talk." Linguists even have a name for this: phatic communication.

The trick is to ask open questions. Instead of "Did you have a good weekend?", which gets a quick "yes", try "What did you get up to at the weekend?" People usually answer with a story, and a story gives you something to react to. Finally, don't worry about silence. A pause of a few seconds feels much longer to you than it does to them.

Comprehension

1. What is small talk really about?

2. What is an open question? Give your own example.

3. Why shouldn't we be afraid of silence?

Critical thinking

Do you agree that small talk is important? When is it easy or hard for you?
